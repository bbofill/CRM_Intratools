Before submitting
  1. Use a Budgeting travel whenever possible.
  2. Submit expenses only after the trip has taken place.
  3. Attach a receipt/invoice and a payment proof for expenses that require them.
  4. For mileage, attach a screenshot showing the kilometers made during the trip.
  5. For accommodation, upload an invoice whenever possible instead of a simple ticket.
  6. For diets, enter departure and return date/time. The amount is calculated from the allowance table.
  7. Use short, clear descriptions.
